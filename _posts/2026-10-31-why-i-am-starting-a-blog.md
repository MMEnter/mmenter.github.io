I've been meaning to write down my thoughts more regularly. Not polished
whitepapers — just ideas I'm chewing on, lessons I've picked up, and opinions
I'm willing to have challenged.

## Why not just post on LinkedIn?

I will. LinkedIn is where the conversation happens, and that's valuable. But a
feed is rented space: the algorithm decides who sees what, the formatting is
limited, and posts get buried in a week.

So the plan is simple:

1. **Write it here first.** This site is the canonical home for everything I publish.
2. **Share it on LinkedIn.** Post the text natively so people can read and react
   where they already are, with a link back here.
3. **Keep it all.** Every post is a plain Markdown file in a Git repository I control.
   No lock-in, no platform risk.

> Own the content, rent the distribution.

## What to expect

Short-to-medium posts, written when I have something worth saying. If something
resonates — or you think I'm wrong — come find me on LinkedIn and tell me.
